RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Company and the Transfer Agent entered into that certain Tax Receivable Agreement dated October 3, 2016 (the “Initial Agreement”);

WHEREAS, a Majority of the Holders provided consent to the amendment and restatement of the Initial Agreement pursuant to the Exchange and Consent Agreement, dated December 29, 2023; and

WHEREAS, the Company and the Transfer Agent wish to effect the amendment and restatement of the Initial Agreement on the terms set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the respective covenants and agreements set forth herein, and intending to be legally bound hereby, the parties hereto agree as follows:
